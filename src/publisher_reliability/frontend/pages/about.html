Publisher Reliability Tool

A research application for classifying news articles and reading a publisher's reliability class from the articles already classified.

Open source Runs on a laptop Plain CSV storage Nothing is ever deleted

What it does

Classify one article from its URL, then read a publisher's class from the articles already classified. That class is not stored: it is computed when you ask for it, under six documented formulas, and every prediction behind it keeps its five probabilities and the exact checkpoint that produced it.

The study

From Articles to Publishers: Aggregating Language Model Predictions for News Source Reliability Inference — Bianchi, Pratelli, Pinelli and Petrocchi, IEEE Transactions on Computational Social Systems, 2026, under review. Preprint on arXiv.

This application is that study's reference implementation, and the subject of a demonstration paper submitted to ECIR 2027.

The release

34,564 predictions over 17,269 English political news articles from 372 publishers, by BERT and RoBERTa under a five-fold, publisher-disjoint protocol. Class 0 to Class 4 are reliability bands in ascending order. The NewsGuard ratings the models were trained on are licensed material and are not redistributed here.

The code

github.com/bianchi-john/publisher-reliability-tool — Apache-2.0.

Every class shown anywhere in this tool is a model prediction — not a fact, a fact check, or a ground-truth rating.
